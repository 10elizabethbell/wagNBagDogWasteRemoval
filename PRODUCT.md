# Product: Wag N' Bag Dog Waste Removal website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), images in `img/`, hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Live:** https://10elizabethbell.github.io/wagNBagDogWasteRemoval/

## Users
- **Primary:** dog owners in the business's (unpublished) service area who want their yard scooped regularly. They find the link on Facebook, on their phones, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- **Secondary:** the owner of Wag N' Bag, who receives the site for free and judges it in the first screen.

## Purpose
Get a dog owner from "I saw your post" to "I've texted you for a free quote" in one screen, and give the business its first web presence.

## Positioning
A friendly, playful pet-waste crew that loves dogs. Their own tagline: "We scoop the poop so you don't have to!" Their seasonal hook: "Why bundle up and tackle those yard bombs when you can stay warm inside and let us handle the dirty work?"

## Capabilities
- Tap-to-call number in the header (609-467-4064, accepts calls and texts: brief, from their ad caption).
- "Text us" and a floating text circle open a pre-written SMS.
- Free-quote form (name, dogs, how often, town, note) composes a text to the business.
- Facebook link under the "why" section and in the footer.

## Constraints
- **No location published** (brief: "Do NOT publish a location"; only the 609 area code is known). So the H1 has no place name, against the calm style's default.
- No email, no prices, no reviews, no owner name, no hours: none were found. Nothing invented.
- Only one photo exists: a frame from their Facebook video ad (yard with a dog).
- Keep clearly distinct from NJ Scatman, another pet-waste business in the pipeline.

## Brand commitments
- Logo colours: umber paw #523c16, taupe stripes #cdc2b2 (sampled from the Facebook profile image).
- Caramel accent #e8a64a is **inferred** (not in the logo) because the calm style needs one warm fill colour; chosen to sit with the umber and stay away from Scatman's mustard.
- Voice: "we", warm and a little playful, their own words wherever possible.

## Evidence on hand
- Facebook profile logo (500×500).
- One video-ad thumbnail (1080×1920) from the Ocean County Home Helpers group post, 2026-10-21.
- Ad caption, quoted verbatim in the brief.

## Principles
1. Phone first: the first phone screen says who, what, and how to get a quote.
2. Their words over ours.
3. Nothing invented: unknowns become questions for the owner in `HANDOFF.md`.
